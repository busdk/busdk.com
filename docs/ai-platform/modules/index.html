Bus AI Platform

Modules behind the AI platform product.

The public product is AI hosting and product infrastructure. Module names explain how the platform is composed for teams that need to evaluate architecture and operations.

Core product modules

  • OpenAI-compatible model and inference provider modules.
  • Deployment, runtime, VM, container, terminal, database, node, and SSH modules.
  • Auth, session, usage, billing, Stripe, catalog, entitlement, and operator modules.
  • Cloud, service, and integration workers that connect platform events to infrastructure.
