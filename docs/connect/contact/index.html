Self-hosting and licensing

The intent is that you run your own instance and are your own service provider, keeping your own initiation fees and your own operational and legal responsibility. The software is source-available under the Functional Source License with a later conversion to a permissive license; the exact terms travel with the source. Whether today's terms permit a given competing commercial use is worth confirming with us before you build on it.

Before you deploy publicly

Taking payment from strangers has consequences beyond code: what you advertise in your terms, how long you retain message content, and what you tell a payer about what their fee does and does not buy. The product keeps those statements narrow on purpose, and a deployment should keep them narrow too.
