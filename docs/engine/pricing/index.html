Named Operator License — unlimited systems

One license is assigned to one natural person who directly administers, operates, or customizes Bus Engine. That named operator may use the preview on an unlimited number of servers, virtual machines, containers, clusters, and other systems for personal or commercial purposes. Hosted application users and billing administrators who only purchase or assign seats do not consume paid operator seats.

Each license includes one individual account for official release access, updates, documentation, and customer issue-tracker support. Shared accounts are not permitted, and organizations may reassign licenses when an employee leaves or changes role.

AI access and support boundary

AI model access is configured separately. Third-party hosted-model usage, OpenAI Codex access, and other AI service fees are not included unless a plan explicitly says otherwise. Customers must provide their own OpenAI Codex access or another model backend supported by the deployed Bus model API and integration modules. Unsupported providers require integration work before they can be used.

Support is best effort during the development preview. There is no production SLA, and rollback, recovery, update, installation, and production image guarantees are provided only after those capabilities are implemented, tested, and included in the applicable release terms.

Source, binaries, and release materials

Bus Engine OS release areas are expected to provide binary artifacts, checksums, package manifests, corresponding source for covered open-source components, patches, build definitions, license texts, copyright notices, third-party notices, and source-access instructions to the release recipients. Bus Engine does not offer source code publicly by default.

Bus-owned source-access components may use the Functional Source License or another published source-access license. Proprietary BusDK userspace products remain binary-only where legally permitted. Open-source component licenses control where they conflict with commercial terms.

Founder persistent storage

The intended founder offer includes a 1 TB Bus Engine Persistent Storage allocation for supported BusDK-hosted virtualized systems, subject to the applicable commercial and service terms. The allocation is meant to remain associated with the customer independently of individual development virtual machine rebuilds and attach through Bus Engine-managed workflows.

Storage is not advertised as lifetime, perpetual, backed up, replicated, or performance-guaranteed on this page. Details such as decimal TB versus TiB, duration, snapshot policy, backup policy, data location, retention after cancellation, export period, transfer limits, and additional-capacity pricing must be confirmed in the applicable agreement before purchase.

Commercial terms still govern

The checkout and written agreement control billing, taxes, renewal, support, and entitlement details. The intended commercial model includes named-operator access, development-channel participation, and access to relevant Bus Engine release materials. Any broader BusDK binary-catalog entitlement must be confirmed in the final commercial terms before it is treated as a contractual deliverable.
