Bus Engine Pricing

One operator. Unlimited systems.

Bus Engine is priced by the people who operate and customize the platform, not by the number of servers, virtual machines, containers, clusters, or other systems they manage. One Named Operator License is assigned to one natural person and may be used on unlimited systems for personal or commercial purposes.

Named Operator License

Each license includes one individual account for official releases, update services, documentation, and customer issue-tracker support. A named operator is someone who directly administers, operates, or customizes Bus Engine. Hosted application users and purchasing-only billing administrators do not consume seats. Shared accounts are not permitted; organizations may reassign licenses when roles change.

AI credits and OpenAI Codex access are not included. Customers provide their own OpenAI Codex subscription or API access for Codex-powered maintenance work. Local AI model use depends on configured Bus LLM and Ollama integrations.

Pricing options

The live checkout shows the current public named-operator prices for Bus Engine. VAT is added at checkout where applicable. Final custom terms, currency handling, and invoice questions can be confirmed through a written sales quote.

Bus Runtime

Official Bus components delivered as binaries, with signed updates, documentation, and runtime support. Third-party source remains available according to each component's license.

Bus Engine Source

Everything in Runtime, plus current Bus Engine, BusDK, and included Engine-module source repositories under FSL, with repository access, version-matched build materials, and source-level support while active.

Bus Engine One-Time

Everything in Bus Engine Source for 12 months. Versions received during that period remain licensed under their included terms and can be used indefinitely.

Account and license boundary

The commercial account entitlement covers the named account, official distribution, update services, documentation, and support access. First-party Bus Engine components remain under their published software license terms, including FSL terms where applicable, and third-party packages remain under their own licenses.

One administrator managing 500 systems needs one license. Five administrators with accounts and support access need five licenses. After monthly cancellation, systems built with eligible releases can continue under the rights that applied to those received releases, but new official releases, updates, restricted documentation, and support require an active subscription or renewal period.

We charge for people we support.

Bus Engine pricing does not count machines. It counts the named operators who need official releases, update services, documentation, and technical support.
