Bus Services Pricing

Choose how your team runs Services.

Bus Services is available as a binary subscription, an FSL source-available subscription that converts to MIT or Apache 2.0 two years after release, or a one-time MIT source release. The binary subscription includes a two-week free trial so teams can validate service stacks before the monthly plan starts.

Compare options Start with Services Contact sales@hg.fi

Purchase options

Binary Subscription

8 €/month after a free two-week trial. Use this when your team wants the supported Bus Services binary for stack validation, planning, startup, status, and shutdown workflows. Source repository access is not included.

FSL Source Subscription

65 €/month for source-available access under FSL terms. Choose this when your team needs GitHub repository access as an outside collaborator, setup instructions, and GitHub Issues support while the subscription is active. Source converts to MIT or Apache 2.0 two years after release.

MIT Source Release

1 305 € one time for an MIT-licensed source release. Use this when you need a permissive source release instead of a recurring source subscription.

Included modules

bus-services

The user-facing Services CLI and profile surface for validating, planning, starting, listing, and stopping service stacks.

bus-api-provider-services

The API-hosted Services lifecycle surface for provider-backed service creation, inspection, and control.

bus-integration-services

The runtime integration layer for local supervision, stack state, status, and process lifecycle behavior.

Start before buying

Prove the operational workflow first: define a stack, run bus services up, inspect with bus services list, and stop with bus services down. If the stack becomes part of a team workflow, continue with the binary subscription or choose source access when your team needs to inspect and adapt the implementation.
