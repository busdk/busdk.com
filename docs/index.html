Business Development Kit

Business software built for AI agents to operate — down to the OS.

BusDK is a self-hostable platform for developing, hosting, billing, and operating AI products. Every product exposes the same CLI, API, and event surfaces to your team and your AI agents alike, so agents can do the operating while people stay in charge: agent workers develop software, AI services run on hardware you control, and the stack goes as deep as a from-source Linux OS maintained by an integrated AI engineering system.
